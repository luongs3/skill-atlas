### Community proposal frameworks (r/Upwork, freelancer blogs)
The genuinely useful tactical patterns (relevance-first, no self-intro, specificity beats
polish) live in community write-ups — but quality varies wildly and most are SEO bait.
- **source:** https://www.reddit.com/r/Upwork/  (sort Top/All-time, read threads not headlines)
- **reputation:** Mixed — individual practitioners, no central authority. Verify each claim.
- **last_validated:** 2026-06-02 (subreddit active)
- **assumes:** nothing
- **adapt:** Harvest patterns, discard generic advice. Cross-check against your own win/loss data.

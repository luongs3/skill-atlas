## Tier B 🔵 — Community-proven

### Moodle
The dominant open-source LMS — courses, quizzes, gradebook, with web-service APIs an agent can drive for content upload and grade export.
- **source:** https://github.com/moodle/moodle
- **reputation:** **7,251★** · pushed 2026-07-14
- **last_validated:** 2026-07-16
- **assumes:** PHP/MySQL or hosted MoodleCloud
- **adapt:** fork your course templates + rubric bank.

### Anki
THE spaced-repetition system. Agents generate decks programmatically (genanki/AnkiConnect) — the highest-leverage 'make study materials' target.
- **source:** https://github.com/ankitects/anki
- **reputation:** **29,162★** · pushed 2026-07-15
- **last_validated:** 2026-07-16
- **assumes:** Anki desktop/mobile; genanki or AnkiConnect for automation
- **adapt:** fork card templates per subject.

### nbgrader
Assignment creation + autograding for Jupyter notebooks — the standard for programming/data courses.
- **source:** https://github.com/jupyter/nbgrader
- **reputation:** **1,370★** · pushed 2026-07-16
- **last_validated:** 2026-07-16
- **assumes:** Jupyter
- **adapt:** fork your assignment templates + test cells.

# Job: Technical Interview Prep

**You're about to:** prep for coding / system-design / behavioral interviews. This job is
**exceptionally** well-served by high-reputation community repos — but several famous ones
are going stale, so tiering on freshness matters here more than anywhere.

> Reputation signals pulled live **2026-06-02**.

*Lesson visible in this job: stars are a lagging signal. A 348k★ repo can be 6 months
stale and a 82k★ repo 2 years dead. The atlas tiers on **stars AND last-push**, which is
why the famous repos land in C/D, not B.*
